# Gifted Motors Records Portal (demo)

A simple website for staff to record money in (hires) and money out (expenses), with a manager overview.

This is the **demo version**: entries stay in the browser and nothing is sent to Google Sheets yet.
Demo passwords: staff `staff`, manager `manager`.

Files: `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`, `logo.png`.
Vehicle and expense-category lists are at the top of `app.js`.
